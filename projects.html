FEATURED PROJECTS

The journey of the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair began in 2025 after witnessing the real-life mobility struggles of a paralysed family member. Since then, Team C.A.R.E has been continuously researching and developing affordable intelligent assistive technology solutions aimed at improving mobility, independence, and quality of life for physically challenged individuals in Bangladesh.
