About Us

WE ARE
TEAM C.A.R.E

Team C.A.R.E is Bangladesh's first nationally and internationally recognized student-led assistive technology innovation team formed by students from Notre Dame College and Adamjee Cantonment College, Dhaka, dedicated to developing intelligent and affordable solutions for physically challenged individuals. The team works on research and innovation in assistive technology, with the C.A.R.E. Wheelchair serving as its flagship project — an AI and IoT-based smart wheelchair designed to enhance mobility, safety, and independence.
